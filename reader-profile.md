## How I Consume Content
- Depth over breadth. Will follow a long argument to the end if the framing earns it. A single well-argued essay beats five hot takes.
- Responds to: bold claims backed by evidence, concrete mechanism explanations, historical analogies used structurally not decoratively.
- Saves and references content that sharpens language for investor or public-facing arguments — content feeds the writing, not just consumption.
- Shares selectively on LinkedIn and X when a piece gives language for something already believed.
- Short takes are low-value unless the idea is genuinely novel. Punchy and opinionated resonates more than hedged corporate prose.
